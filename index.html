A New Generation of AMD Instinct™ Performance

AMD Instinct™ MI355 vs. MI325

Built on AMD CDNA™ 4 architecture, the AMD Instinct™ MI355X advances AI computing with faster GPU-to-GPU communication, expanded HBM3E memory, and nearly 2× FP8 compute throughput compared with the previous generation. With native MXFP6 and MXFP4 support, MI355X further strengthens low-precision AI processing capabilities, offering a compelling compute option for large-scale generative AI and inference workloads.

Eight AMD Instinct MI355X GPUs

Built for large-scale AI inference and training

Featuring eight AMD Instinct™ MI355X accelerators based on AMD CDNA™ 4 architecture, XA AM3A-E13 offers up to 288GB of HBM3E memory and 8TB/s bandwidth per GPU, providing the computational resources to handle increasingly complex AI models and memory-intensive workloads.

Balanced GPU-to-NIC Connectivity

1:1:1 connection between storage, GPU and NIC

XA AM3A-E13 features a 1:1:1 connectivity architecture linking storage, GPUs and NICs through PCIe 5.0 switches. Combined with AMD Infinity Fabric™ interconnects across eight MI355X GPUs, this design facilitates efficient data movement and high-bandwidth communication for distributed AI training and data-intensive workloads.

AMD ROCm™ open software platform

An open ecosystem for AI development and deployment

Powered by the AMD ROCm™ software ecosystem, the AMD Instinct™ MI355X GPUs in ASUS XA AM3A-E13 offer compatibility with leading AI frameworks and inference engines, including PyTorch, TensorFlow, JAX, vLLM and SGLang. ROCm simplifies model migration, training and inference workflows, helping developers deploy AI applications with minimal code changes across enterprise AI and HPC environments.

FAQs

  • What are the key features of the AMD Instinct™ MI355X GPUs in ASUS XA AM3A-E13?
    What are the key features of the AMD Instinct™ MI355X GPUs in ASUS XA AM3A-E13? ASUS XA AM3A-E13 features eight AMD Instinct™ MI355X GPUs built on AMD CDNA™ 4 architecture. Each GPU offers 288GB of HBM3E memory, up to 8TB/s memory bandwidth, and support for AI-optimized precision formats including MXFP4 and MXFP6. The eight-GPU platform provides 2.3TB of aggregate GPU memory for large-scale AI inference, training, and HPC workloads.
  • What is the difference between AMD Instinct™ MI325X and MI355X GPUs?
    What is the difference between AMD Instinct™ MI325X and MI355X GPUs? Compared with MI325X, AMD Instinct™ MI355X introduces AMD CDNA™ 4 architecture, increases HBM3E memory from 256GB to 288GB per GPU, and boosts memory bandwidth from 6TB/s to 8TB/s. MI355X also adds MXFP4 and MXFP6 support, offering greater flexibility for low-precision AI computing and increasingly complex generative AI workloads.
  • Does ASUS XA AM3A-E13 require liquid cooling for its eight AMD MI355X GPUs?
    Does ASUS XA AM3A-E13 require liquid cooling for its eight AMD MI355X GPUs? No. ASUS XA AM3A-E13 features a purpose-built 10U air-cooled architecture that accommodates eight AMD Instinct™ MI355X GPUs and dual AMD EPYC™ processors without requiring direct liquid cooling. This design offers an alternative for organizations seeking to deploy advanced AI computing capabilities within existing air-cooled data centers, subject to facility power and thermal requirements.
  • What AI workloads is ASUS XA AM3A-E13 designed for?
    What AI workloads is ASUS XA AM3A-E13 designed for? ASUS XA AM3A-E13 is designed for generative AI, large language model (LLM) inference, AI training, and high-performance computing (HPC). Its eight-GPU architecture, substantial HBM3E memory capacity, and PCIe 5.0 connectivity provide the computational and data-handling resources needed for memory-intensive models, scientific simulations, and enterprise AI applications.
  • What makes ASUS XA AM3A-E13 easier to maintain?
    What makes ASUS XA AM3A-E13 easier to maintain? ASUS XA AM3A-E13 features a modular, service-friendly design that simplifies hardware maintenance. Clearly marked service access points help IT technicians quickly identify components, while tool-free access to fan modules, NIC risers, and front I/O modules streamlines replacement and servicing. This design helps reduce maintenance complexity and improve operational efficiency in demanding data center environments.
